ABOUT

SELECTED RESEARCH & PUBLICATIONS

DISTMM — NSDI 2024
DISTMM: Accelerating Distributed Multimodal Model Training
A distributed training system for multimodal models, using modality-aware parallelization, load balancing, and topology-aware placement. DISTMM-Pipe addresses the interaction-batch-size limitation of conventional pipeline parallelism for contrastive learning.
Paper: https://www.usenix.org/conference/nsdi24/presentation/huang

CoCoNet — ASPLOS 2022
Breaking the Computation and Communication Abstraction Barrier in Distributed Machine Learning Workloads
A framework for generating fused computation and communication kernels to overlap collective operations with pointwise computation and reductions. This work was integrated into MSCCL and deployed in Azure.
Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.05720

LOCA — Under submission, 2026
LOCA: Accelerating Video Generation with the Localities in Diffusion
Accelerates video diffusion inference by combining cross-frame and cross-step locality optimizations with an adaptive, learning-based fusion strategy and a compatible execution schedule.
